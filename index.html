How trading here works

Digit contracts pay out based on the last digit of a price. Over and under compare it with a barrier you choose. Even and odd check whether it's even or odd.

Rise and fall pay out if the price ends higher or lower after a few ticks.

Each contract has a normal win rate, and Deriv sets its payout to match. The scanner measures how far each market drifts from its normal rate and scores it from 0 to 100. A high score means the market is behaving unusually. It does not mean the next contract will win, because these markets are generated at random.

Trading is high risk. You can lose some or all of the money you trade. Only trade what you can afford to lose. Trade responsibly and don't overtrade. Test on a demo account first.
