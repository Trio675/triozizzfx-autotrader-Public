Quick guides

Install an Expert Advisor in MetaTrader 5
  1. Open MT5 → File → Open Data Folder.
  2. Open MQL5 → Experts and paste the EA file there.
  3. Restart MT5 (or right-click Expert Advisors in the Navigator → Refresh).
  4. Drag the EA onto a chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, set your inputs, and press OK.
  5. Turn on the Algo Trading button in the toolbar. Test on a demo account first.
Risk management basics
  • Risk a small, fixed part of your balance per trade, often 1–2%.
  • Always use a stop loss, and set a daily loss limit you won't break.
  • Martingale grows stakes fast: plan the worst losing streak before you start.
  • Never trade money you can't afford to lose.
Trading psychology
  • Follow your plan. Don't chase losses with bigger stakes.
  • Stop for the day after hitting your target or your limit.
  • Keep a journal: what you traded, why, and what happened.
How Deriv digit contracts work
  • Digit contracts pay out based on the last digit of the price after a set number of ticks.
  • Over/Under with a high chance (like Over 1) pays little; a low chance (like Over 8) pays more but loses most of the time.
  • Even/Odd is close to 50/50. Deriv's payout means you need to win a bit over half the time to break even.
