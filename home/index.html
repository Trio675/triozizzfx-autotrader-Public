Quick guides

Install an Expert Advisor in MetaTrader 5
  1. Open MT5 → File → Open Data Folder.
  2. Open MQL5 → Experts and paste the EA file there.
  3. Restart MT5 (or right-click Expert Advisors in the Navigator → Refresh).
  4. Drag the EA onto a chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, set your inputs, and press OK.
  5. Turn on the Algo Trading button in the toolbar. Test on a demo account first.
Risk management basics
  • Risk a small, fixed part of your balance per trade, often 1–2%.
  • Always use a stop loss, and set a daily loss limit you won't break.
  • Martingale grows stakes fast: plan the worst losing streak before you start.
  • Never trade money you can't afford to lose.
Trading psychology
  • Follow your plan. Don't chase losses with bigger stakes.
  • Stop for the day after hitting your target or your limit.
  • Keep a journal: what you traded, why, and what happened.
How Deriv digit contracts work
  • Digit contracts pay out based on the last digit of the price after a set number of ticks.
  • Over/Under with a high chance (like Over 1) pays little; a low chance (like Over 8) pays more but loses most of the time.
  • Even/Odd is close to 50/50. Deriv's payout means you need to win a bit over half the time to break even.

Questions and answers

How do I connect my Deriv account?

Open the Auto-Trader, press Connect and choose Log in with Deriv. You sign in on Deriv's own page and come straight back. Or use your API token and app ID from the Deriv developer dashboard.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool in the Auto-Trader and every EA download is free. Real-money trades through the TRIOZIZZFX Deriv app include Deriv's 3% app fee in the price; demo and paper trades have none.

What's free and what needs a plan?

Free: connecting, balances, history, risk rules and the Six Step Cycle bot on demo or paper. Everything else (engine, all other bots, signals, markets, Strategy Lab, Auto bot, copy trading) needs a plan.

I paid. How do I unlock the app?

The phone or computer you paid on unlocks by itself. We also email a 6-digit access code: open the Auto-Trader → Account → My access, enter your email and the code. A code works once, on one device. Lost it? Tap Send me a code.

Can I use my plan on two phones?

One device at a time. Unlocking a new device signs out the old one.

The M-Pesa prompt didn't come. What now?

Check the number (07… or 01…), make sure your phone is on and unlocked, and wait a minute before trying again. If money left your M-Pesa but the app didn't unlock, send us the M-Pesa message on WhatsApp.

How do I install an EA?

Copy the EA into MT5 → File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 → Experts and restart MT5. Drag the EA onto a chart, tick Allow Algo Trading and turn on the Algo Trading button.

I changed my MT5 account. Can I move my licence?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your licence key and the new account number.

Do you hold my money?

No. Your money stays in your own Deriv or broker account. The Auto-Trader places trades on your account only while you run it, and you can stop it any time.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are non-refundable (see the Terms below). Test the free Six Step Cycle bot and the demo account first.
