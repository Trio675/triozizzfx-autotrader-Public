# TRIOZIZZFX Auto-Trader

A browser app for trading Deriv synthetic indices with live data, clear rules and honest statistics.

**Live site:** https://functional-website-dba168.netlify.app

## What's inside

- **Engine** – scans markets patiently and trades only what passes your confidence and risk rules.
- **Signals** – live digit statistics for every market, compared with normal rates.
- **XML bots** – run Deriv Bot (DBot) XML strategies, plus 20+ built-in TZ bots.
- **Digit signals & Top 3 autopick** – live top picks across markets, and a bot that trades them in cycles.
- **Auto bot loader** – trades the #1 market from the Signals page.
- **Strategy Lab** – backtest on real Deriv tick history with Deriv's payouts.
- **Copy trading** – mirror your trades to up to 10 accounts you're trusted with (tokens are encrypted with a PIN on your device).
- **Markets** – live prices, last digits, and streaming tick and candle charts.
- **Insights & Risk rules** – edge monitor, confidence check, trading hours, cool-down, profit lock, drawdown brake and more.
- **Paper mode** – practise with live prices and no real trades.

## How it runs

Everything is one static page (`index.html`). Your Deriv tokens stay in your own browser: nothing is sent to any server except Deriv's.

## Deploy

Connected to Netlify: every push to `main` goes live automatically.

## Risk warning

Trading synthetic indices is high risk. Results shown are past performance and do not predict the future. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
